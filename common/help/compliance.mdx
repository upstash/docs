## Is Upstash SOC 2 Compliant?

Yes. Upstash has a SOC 2 Type II report. SOC 2 controls apply across the
platform, and the report is available to databases with the
[Prod Pack add-on](/redis/overall/enterprise#prod-pack-features) and to
Enterprise customers. You can request it through the
[Trust Center](https://trust.upstash.com/).

## Is Upstash ISO 27001 Compliant?

We are in the process of getting this certification. Contact us
([support@upstash.com](mailto:support@upstash.com)) to learn about the expected
date.

## Is Upstash GDPR Compliant?

Yes. For more information, see our
[Privacy Policy](https://upstash.com/static/trust/privacy.pdf) and
[Data Processing Agreement](https://upstash.com/static/trust/dpa.pdf). We
acquire DPAs from each
[subcontractor](https://upstash.com/static/trust/subprocessors.pdf) that we
work with. Upstash also participates in the Data Privacy Framework (DPF).

## Is Upstash HIPAA Compliant?

Yes. Upstash Redis is HIPAA compliant and you can use it to store and process
Protected Health Information (PHI). A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and
HIPAA enablement are available with the
[Enterprise plan](/redis/overall/enterprise#hipaa-compliance). See
[Managing Healthcare Data](/redis/help/managing-healthcare-data) for your
responsibilities, and contact us
([support@upstash.com](mailto:support@upstash.com)) to get started.

## Is Upstash PCI Compliant?

Upstash does not store personal credit card information. We use Stripe for
payment processing. Stripe is a certified PCI Service Provider Level 1, which is
the highest level of certification in the payments industry.

## Does Upstash conduct vulnerability scanning and penetration tests?

Yes, we use third party tools and work with pen testers. We share the results
with Enterprise customers. Contact us
([support@upstash.com](mailto:support@upstash.com)) for more information.

## Does Upstash take backups?

Yes. We take regular snapshots of the data cluster for disaster recovery. You
can also create backups of your own databases, schedule daily backups and
restore them. See [Backup/Restore](/redis/features/backup). Hourly backups with
custom retention are available for Enterprise customers.

## Does Upstash encrypt data?

Yes. TLS is always enabled on Upstash Redis databases, so data in transit
between your client and the database is encrypted. Encryption at rest is
available with the [Prod Pack add-on](/redis/overall/enterprise#prod-pack-features)
and included in the Enterprise plan. See [Security](/redis/features/security).
