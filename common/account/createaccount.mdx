---
title: Create an Account
---

You can sign up for <a href="https://console.upstash.com" target="_blank">Upstash</a> using your Amazon, Github or Google accounts. Alternatively, if you prefer not to use these authentication providers or want to sign up with a corporate email address, you can also sign up using email and password.

<Note>
  One account covers every Upstash product. After signing up, the same account
  gives you access to Redis, QStash, Vector, Box, Workflow and the other tabs in
  the console — you do not need a separate account or sign-up per product. Each
  product has its own usage-based pricing, but usage of all of them is billed to
  the same account and payment method. See [Billing
  FAQ](/common/account/faq) and the pricing page of each product for details.
</Note>

<Note>

We do not access your information other than:

- Your email
- Your name
- Your profile picture and we never share your information with third parties.

</Note>
